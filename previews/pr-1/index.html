OUR APPROACH

Better materials.
Fewer compromises.

Good products start with good materials. We select components for durability, consistency, and long-term use rather than simply choosing the lowest-cost option.

When practical, Lat39 Designs prioritizes American-made materials and components from established manufacturers.

ABOUT LAT39

Designed with a purpose.

Handcrafted in Kansas

Lat39 Designs LLC is a small product design and manufacturing company based in Kansas.

Every product starts with a problem worth solving. Ideas move from sketch, to prototype, to hands-on testing, to a repeatable manufacturing process before becoming a finished Lat39 Designs product.

The goal is simple: useful equipment that does its job well and holds up to real-world use.
